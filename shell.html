Every play is a masterclass in English — hiding inside a story you'll actually want to read.

Grammar drills fade from memory. Stories don't. Each of Shakespeare's 37 plays is retold here in plain, powerful English — built to sharpen your vocabulary, your sentence sense, and your instinct for how a story is built, one page at a time.

This isn't Shakespeare translated into boredom. It's ambition, love, betrayal, and forgiveness, still asking the same questions four centuries later — and a way into English that teaches you to write, not just to memorise.
